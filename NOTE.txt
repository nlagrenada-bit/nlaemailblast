Lotto and Super 6 draw time: 8:30pm -> 7:45pm
==============================================

FILE CHANGED:  src/components/Rail.jsx   (one file, nothing else)

Two edits:

1. Both draws changed from time: '20:30' to time: '19:45'.

2. Moved the stops.sort() call so it runs AFTER Lotto and Super 6 are added,
   not before. Previously the sort ran first, which happened to look right only
   because 20:30 was later than everything else. At 19:45 they would have been
   stranded at the bottom of the rail, below the 8:45pm Night Pop.

   A tiebreak was added so the three 7:45pm draws always appear in the same
   order: Evening Draw, then Lotto, then Super 6.
